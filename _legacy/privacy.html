Privacy policy

How TrustLed AI handles the information you share through this website.

This policy explains how TrustLed AI Ltd ("TrustLed AI", "we") collects and uses personal information through trustledai.com. TrustLed AI Ltd is registered in England and Wales (No. 17174921) and in Nigeria (CAC No. 9613640).

What we collect

When you complete a form on this website, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, organisation and message.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or request
  • To send updates you have asked to receive, which you can stop at any time
  • To manage early access to our products

How your information reaches us

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox at hello@trustledai.com through FormSubmit, a form delivery service. We do not sell your information or share it for marketing by others.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as we need it to respond and maintain a record of our correspondence.

Your rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. To make a request, email hello@trustledai.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
