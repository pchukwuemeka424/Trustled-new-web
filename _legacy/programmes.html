For institutions

University AI Capability & Integrity Programme (UAICIP)

UAICIP equips an entire campus community to use AI with skill and integrity. Every participant completes a Shared Foundation, then follows a track built for their role. Each track ends with a final assessment that certifies what participants have learned.

The programme has been delivered across 10 universities and Federal Colleges of Education in Nigeria.
