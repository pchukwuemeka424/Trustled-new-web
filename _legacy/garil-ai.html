Capabilities

Four capabilities, one governed environment

01

Academic Contribution and Provenance

Students record observations, readings, interviews and drafts as they work, creating a traceable record of their own contribution. At the end of a research sprint, an effort contribution record can be submitted with the final work.

02

AI Research Assist

Help across the research lifecycle, grounded in the user's own records and recognised databases such as Google Scholar, arXiv, PubMed and CORE.

03

AI-Assisted Supervision

Structured support for supervisors and examiners, from topic approval through chapter review to final submission.

04

Evidence-Backed Governance

Usage analytics, audit trails and policy controls, with reporting formatted for management, Senate and external auditors.
