The programmes

Learning matched to each role in your organisation

Our Training and Certification Programmes give every person in your organisation the knowledge their role demands, from the board member approving an AI strategy to the officer drafting a report with a chatbot open beside them. Each programme blends live sessions, practical exercises drawn from your own work and a final assessment, so completion means something you can measure.

Participants who pass receive a TrustLed AI certificate. You receive a record of who was trained, on what and to which standard, the evidence regulators and funders now expect.
