About the assessment

A quick measure before anyone else asks

Most organisations cannot say with any certainty how AI is used across their teams, and the first time they try is often when a client, regulator or funder asks. This assessment gives you that answer early, in a form you can share with leadership.

It scores you across ownership, visibility, data handling, review, and training and response, the five areas we see decide whether AI use holds up under scrutiny. The questions draw on ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF, UK GDPR and Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, and your answers stay in your browser unless you choose to send them.
