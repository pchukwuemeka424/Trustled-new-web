About TrustLed AI

We started TrustLed AI after watching organisations take up AI faster than they could govern it. Our team combines governance, security, research and education experience, and we work with universities and research institutes, businesses and public sector bodies to put AI to work with confidence.

Governance Policy Training Research Tools
Our mission

To help organisations adopt AI with clear governance, capable people and tools they can trust.

A future where every organisation using AI can show that it does so safely, fairly and to good effect.
