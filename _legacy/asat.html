ASAT. The AI Self-Assessment Toolkit.

A guided platform for organisations to understand every AI system they use, the risks each one carries and the obligations that apply, then act on what they find.

How it will work

Six steps from first sight to fixed

  1. 01

    Intake

    Capture each new AI tool or use case through one consistent request form.

  2. 02

    Inventory

    Keep a single register of every AI system, its owner and the data it touches.

  3. 03

    Classification

    Rate each system's risk level using criteria drawn from recognised frameworks.

  4. 04

    Obligations

    See which requirements apply under ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF, the EU AI Act and GDPR.

  5. 05

    Assessment

    Answer guided questions that measure how well current controls meet those requirements.

  6. 06

    Remediation

    Turn every gap into a tracked action with an owner and a deadline.
