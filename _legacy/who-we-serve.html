Three sectors. One standard of responsible AI.

A university, a growing business and a government agency each answer to different rules, people and pressures. We shape every engagement around the realities of the sector we are working in.

Universities and research institutes

Students and staff are already using AI in coursework, research and administration. Institutions need rules that protect academic integrity and tools that make good practice the easy choice.

Case study: UAICIP across 10 institutions
How we help
  • Institutional AI policies and academic integrity frameworks
  • UAICIP training for students, academic staff and administrators
  • GARIL AI, our governed workspace for research and learning
  • Reporting for management, Senate and external reviewers

Businesses

Teams adopt AI tools quickly to save time, while customers, regulators and contracts expect firm control over data. Businesses need governance that keeps both sides satisfied.

Case study: Acceptable AI Use Policy
How we help
  • Acceptable AI use policies and tool approval routes
  • AI risk assessments and ISO/IEC 42001 readiness
  • Training for leadership and staff
  • Custom AI tools built with governance controls

Public sector bodies

Public agencies carry a duty of fairness and transparency to every citizen they serve. AI in public services needs clear accountability, careful procurement and staff who understand the risks.

How we help
  • Responsible AI frameworks and policy guidance
  • Procurement criteria and risk reviews for AI suppliers
  • Capability building for public servants
  • Public programmes on responsible AI adoption

Our training has also reached teams working in health, energy and climate.
